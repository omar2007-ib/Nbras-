# Nibras Learning Design Skill

## Learning model
- Organize learning from تأسيس to guided practice to timed practice to mixed review.
- Preserve the two course names and slogans: تأسيس — «تأسيس من حديد»؛ تدريبات — «الخطوة الأخيرة».
- Every lesson or practice set should state its objective, prerequisites when relevant, estimated time, and completion criteria.
- Prefer worked examples, gradual release (example → guided question → independent question), spaced review, and mistake analysis.
- Quantitative topics may include arithmetic, ratios, percentages, algebra, geometry, statistics, probability, and word problems; do not imply a topic is covered unless its content exists.
- Keep Saudi Qudrat terminology clear and consistent. Avoid unexplained jargon.

## Study plans
- Create plans from available time, target date, topics, and learner performance only when those inputs exist.
- Alternate topics and revisit weak areas; include short review blocks and rest.
- Distinguish a suggested plan from a guaranteed score improvement.
- Persist progress only through a real backend or clearly explain local-only browser storage.

## Assessment design
- Use one unambiguous stem, plausible distractors, and exactly one defensible correct answer for single-answer MCQs.
- Write a concise explanation for the correct answer and, where useful, why common distractors are wrong.
- Tag questions by topic, skill, difficulty, source, and review status when that metadata is available.
- Avoid copying protected questions or answer keys without permission.
- Never fabricate an official exam question or claim that generated questions are official.

## Acceptance checks
- Learners can identify what to study next and why.
- Instructions are short and readable on iPad and mobile.
- Practice feedback explains the concept, not just right/wrong.
- Completion and score summaries use transparent calculations.
